# Design language

The world is stationery, not terrain.

| Page | Paper | Layout idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cover | Blue lines, red margin, spiral holes | Arrival plaza. Coffee ring is the fountain. |
| Graph paper | Green grid and axes | Geometry village. Rooms are plotted shapes. |
| Comic | Heavy ink panels | Frames you walk through like rooms. |
| Pocket | Manila folder | Paperclip park and a crumpled-ball courtyard. |
| Back page | Warm, torn | Yearbook wall and leftover exam panic. |

Places are drawings you can stand in. The HUD names them. Notes stick to that sheet only.
