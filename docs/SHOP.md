# Ink and the shop

Currency is **ink**. A new name starts with 40.

Earn it by talking (+1 per chat) and hanging out (+2 per minute).

Walk to the **Ink shop** page. Stand at the counter. Buy hats, ink colors, glasses, a scarf, or a backpack. Wallets save under your name in `data/wallets.json`.

Free looks: none, cap, bow, antenna, and four starter colors. Everything else costs ink.
